Boss 1

Rip the bosses apart

You are a beast with a chainsaw for a right arm. Pick where the saw bites: cut the ARM to blunt a boss, the LEGS to slow it, the HEAD or BODY to finish it.

Dash through swings. Jump the red shockwaves. Every boss kill levels you up and charges an ULT: a saw frenzy that tears off half a boss's health.

Keys: A/D move, W jump, Shift dash, 1 2 3 4 = head, body, arm, legs, Q = ult.
